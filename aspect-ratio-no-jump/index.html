How we made our blog load 2× faster

6 min read · @splearncss

Readers kept losing their place. They started reading, an image arrived, and the whole article slid down the screen.

The fix was not a faster server. It was telling the browser how much space the image needs before it loads.

Turn aspect-ratio off, then tick "Load the image": the text jumps. Turn it on: the space is saved and nothing moves.
